# Content Creation Templates

## Recognition Signals

- Draft, audience, message, outline, copy, publishing, interview, announcement.
- Choose this family when the output is content to publish, present, or adapt.

## Core Templates

### article-draft

Fields: audience, thesis, outline, key arguments, examples, ending, next edit.
Recommended components: `InsightCard`, `ReferenceList`, `Checklist`.
Use when: drafting blogs, newsletters, public notes, or long-form posts.
Avoid when: the purpose is private learning; use learning-notes.

### talk-ppt-outline

Fields: audience, objective, narrative arc, slide outline, speaker notes, examples.
Recommended components: `Timeline`, `Checklist`, `QuoteCard`.
Use when: preparing a speech, deck, or lecture.
Avoid when: recording notes from someone else's talk; use learning-notes.

### social-content-plan

Fields: audience, platform, angle, content pieces, schedule, measurement.
Recommended components: `Kanban`, `ActionTable`, `StatusBadge`.
Use when: planning social posts or campaign content.
Avoid when: it is an operations event plan; use hr-operations.

### product-copy

Fields: audience, offer, value proposition, proof, tone, variants, decision.
Recommended components: `OptionMatrix`, `QuoteCard`, `DecisionRecord`.
Use when: creating copy for products, announcements, landing pages, or release notes.
Avoid when: the material is a PRD; use project-docs.

### interview-record

Fields: subject, context, key quotes, themes, usable excerpts, follow-up.
Recommended components: `Transcript`, `QuoteCard`, `ReferenceList`.
Use when: preparing or processing interviews for publication.
Avoid when: the interview is customer research; use research-analysis.

## Subtype Variants

| Subtype | Apply changes |
|---|---|
| press-release | Add headline, lead, quote, availability, boilerplate |
| announcement | Lead with what changed and who is affected |
| speaker-notes | Add timing, transitions, emphasis |
| newsletter-brief | Add sections, links, call to action |

## Quality Rules

- Keep audience and objective visible.
- Separate raw material from publishable copy.
- Preserve reusable quotes with source.
