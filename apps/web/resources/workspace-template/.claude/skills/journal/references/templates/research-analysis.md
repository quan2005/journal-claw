# Research Analysis Templates

## Recognition Signals

- Research question, findings, data, evidence, user sample, market, competitor, hypothesis.
- Choose this family when the note turns source material into conclusions.

## Core Templates

### market-research

Fields: question, market context, segments, findings, evidence, opportunities, risks.
Recommended components: `InsightCard`, `EvidenceCard`, `BarChart`, `RiskMatrix`.
Use when: market or industry context is central.
Avoid when: comparing specific products only; use competitor-analysis.

### competitor-analysis

Fields: competitors, comparison dimensions, strengths, weaknesses, positioning, opportunities.
Recommended components: `ComparisonMatrix`, `EvidenceCard`, `InsightCard`.
Use when: multiple products or vendors are compared.
Avoid when: comparing purchase options for personal use; use personal-journal.

### data-analysis

Fields: question, data scope, metric changes, explanation, anomalies, recommendations.
Recommended components: `StatGroup`, `LineChart`, `BarChart`, `EvidenceCard`.
Use when: numerical evidence is central.
Avoid when: data is only status reporting; use work-reports.

### user-research

Fields: sample, method, pain points, quotes, insights, opportunities, follow-up.
Recommended components: `QuoteCard`, `EvidenceCard`, `InsightCard`, `ActionTable`.
Use when: interviews, surveys, or user behavior evidence are central.
Avoid when: it is a single customer-success status note; use hr-operations or meeting-collaboration.

### risk-assessment

Fields: context, risk list, likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner, monitoring signal.
Recommended components: `RiskMatrix`, `ActionTable`, `DecisionRecord`.
Use when: identifying and prioritizing risk is the main task.
Avoid when: risks are just one section of another template.

## Subtype Variants

| Subtype              | Apply changes                                              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| swot                 | Use strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats          |
| feasibility-analysis | Add constraints, feasibility judgment, required validation |
| experiment-report    | Add hypothesis, experiment design, result, decision        |
| business-analysis    | Add business model, cost, revenue, strategic implication   |

## Quality Rules

- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Preserve source, sample size, and data scope.
- Do not convert weak evidence into strong conclusions.
