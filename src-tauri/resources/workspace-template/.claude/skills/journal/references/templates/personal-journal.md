# Personal Journal Templates

## Recognition Signals

- Personal reflection, mood, habits, travel, buying decision, family matter, personal goal.
- Choose this family for life records even though the product remains professional and quiet.

## Core Templates

### daily-journal

Fields: events, mood, key thought, meaning, next small action.
Recommended components: `Section`, `Callout`, `ActionTable`.
Use when: recording personal day-to-day experience.
Avoid when: the note is mostly work reporting; use work-reports.

### review-journal

Fields: goal, result, reason, lesson, next adjustment.
Recommended components: `InsightCard`, `ActionTable`, `Progress`.
Use when: reviewing a day, week, month, quarter, year, habit, or life phase.
Avoid when: reviewing a project; use project-docs.

### goal-okr

Fields: objective, key results, current progress, confidence, risks, next actions.
Recommended components: `Progress`, `ActionTable`, `RiskMatrix`.
Use when: personal goals or OKRs are central.
Avoid when: it is a company OKR update; use work-reports.

### decision-journal

Fields: decision, options, criteria, tradeoffs, assumption, expected result, review date.
Recommended components: `DecisionRecord`, `ComparisonMatrix`, `RiskMatrix`.
Use when: making or reviewing an important personal decision.
Avoid when: the decision belongs to a team meeting; use meeting-collaboration.

### personal-plan

Fields: goal, itinerary or schedule, checklist, budget or constraints, risks.
Recommended components: `Checklist`, `ActionTable`, `ComparisonMatrix`.
Use when: planning travel, family affairs, purchases, or personal projects.
Avoid when: it is a project plan for work; use project-docs.

## Subtype Variants

| Subtype | Apply changes |
|---|---|
| morning-journal | Emphasize intention, energy, top priorities |
| evening-journal | Emphasize events, reflection, closure |
| emotion-log | Add trigger, body signal, interpretation, regulation |
| habit-tracking | Use progress, streak, blocker, next adjustment |
| travel-plan | Add itinerary, transport, lodging, budget, checklist |
| purchase-decision | Use `ComparisonMatrix` and `DecisionRecord` |
| family-affairs | Add people, timeline, responsibility, follow-up |

## Quality Rules

- Keep personal writing calm and precise, not therapeutic performance.
- Do not infer emotions beyond source evidence.
- Treat private details with restraint.
